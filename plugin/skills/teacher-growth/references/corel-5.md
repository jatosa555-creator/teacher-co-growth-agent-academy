# CoRel-5 | optional Human–AI Collaboration Reflection

The project notes describe CoRel-5 separately from Teacher 5 Shifts. This lens reflects **the evolving relationship with AI**, not the impact of teaching practice. Its underlying source was not verified for this release.

1. Dependence — AI answers ad hoc tasks, little reflection or review.
2. Co-Use — purposeful use of prompt/templates, primarily as a tool.
3. Co-Creation — iterative human–AI co-design using structured feedback.
4. Co-Learning — meta-reflection; reciprocal learning about strategies, strengths and limits.
5. Co-Growth — sustained reflection–feedback–redesign patterns with human ownership.

### Use carefully
Only invoke when the teacher asks to reflect on AI collaboration. Ask about actual examples; never label a teacher at a fixed level from one chat or invent numerical maturity scores. Frame as 'current evidence suggests' and invite teacher correction.
