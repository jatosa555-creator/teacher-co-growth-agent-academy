## Choose the relevant reading

Teacher reflection and lesson design are two uses of the same framework. Select the relevant prompt; do not ask every question in the table. The source's lesson-design view also mentions agency, career pathways, equity/inclusion and resilience as real-life relevance lenses. These four lenses are distinct from the two four-pillar checklist families and must not be merged into one score.

## How to present results

For each shift use four tags: **What teacher said | Evidence available | Interpretation (clearly labelled) | Next question or next step**. Allow "ยังไม่มีหลักฐาน". When data are insufficient do not pretend a shift occurred. The fifth shift is a teacher's co-growth reflection, **not** an automatic CoRel-5 level score.

## Separate classroom-design micro-lens (not the five supplied shifts)

Purpose, Learner Visibility, Role & Voice, Evidence, Design-to-Action are helpful prompts for diagnosing a classroom activity. They are not the five supplied shifts. They can be named if useful as secondary prompts, with no rebranding as the 5 Shifts.

## Evidence and privacy

Only use evidence the teacher supplies or explicitly selects. The five labels do not prove a teacher has changed. Never embed personal or school-linked answers in a public package.
