# Source and framework notes

This portable package uses the five-shift labels and two distinct checklist families described in the project notes. The underlying training artifacts were not independently checked as part of this release. Source notes also contain a discrepancy about the workshop date; do not cite a precise date as verified.

## Framework separation

- **Teacher 5 Shifts:** Perspective, Affective, Process Thinking, System, Co-Growth. Use as reflection prompts, not a validated score or proof of individual change.
- **Classroom Practical Checklist:** a classroom inclusion, practice, SEL, and resilience lens.
- **AI Teacher Practice Checklist:** a separate responsible AI, human learning, resilience, and future pathways lens.
- **CoRel-5:** a separate human–AI collaboration reflection model. Its stages are not the Teacher 5 Shifts.

Use a checklist supplied by the teacher when they want assessment against an official instrument. The condensed references in this package are illustrative prompts, not complete reproductions or validated instruments.

## Evidence handling

Use only artifacts the teacher selects and provides or explicitly authorizes through a connected host tool. No private source is searched automatically. Do not ingest or package raw identifiable teacher/student records. For any reviewed source, disclose the title, locator, and whether coverage was full, partial, or unverified. A source map or filename is not proof that its contents were inspected.
